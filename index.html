About Me

SEO that starts with understanding the business.

I believe effective SEO is more than building links or adding keywords. It starts with understanding a website, its audience and the search intent behind each page.

I'm Toshif Khan, an independent SEO Specialist providing on-page and off-page SEO services. I work with businesses and agencies to improve website structure, content relevance, search visibility and backlink quality.

My approach is focused on relevant websites, thoughtful optimization and consistent reporting—not automated link blasts or shortcuts. Every project should have a clear plan based on its goals and current website condition.

I work directly with clients, so communication stays simple and project updates stay transparent.
